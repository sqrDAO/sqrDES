# Brand Assets

Most sqrDAO ecosystem logos are hosted on the official site and are **not** duplicated here. Download them from:

**[sqrdao.com/brand-kit](https://www.sqrdao.com/brand-kit)**

Available there in **SVG** and **PNG**, dark and light variants: sqrDAO, sqrFUND, sqrVOICE, sqrNODE, sqrBUIDL, Web3 Builders' Summit.

## What this repo does hold

Two sub-brands ship canonical files in-repo because they are not on the brand-kit site. Use these directly rather than redrawing:

| Sub-brand | Location | Files |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Da Nang Blockchain Hub | `assets/danang-hub/logos/` | `Main_Dark.svg`, `Main_Light.svg`, `Mono_Dark.svg`, `Mono_Light.svg`. **The name is the ink, not the canvas:** `*_Dark.svg` is near-black `#231F20` ink for cream/white backgrounds, `*_Light.svg` is cream `#FFF2DD` ink for dark backgrounds. This is the opposite of the sqrDAO brand-kit convention in [BRAND-KIT.md](../BRAND-KIT.md); do not carry that convention over. |
| EMpower Founders Residency | `empower-residency/public/brand/` | `logo-a.svg` (full lockup), `topo-lines.svg` (decorative, `stroke="currentColor"`). Other variants are still pending — see [empower-residency/TODO-assets.md](../empower-residency/TODO-assets.md) |

Raw Figma exports are kept out of git and out of any served directory; see `empower-residency/design-source/README.md`.

Logo usage rules (clear space, no modification, no conflicting backgrounds) are in [BRAND-KIT.md](../BRAND-KIT.md).
